# Sources and lore policy

The project uses whole attested forenames and selected Khazalid senses from official publications. Complete generated compounds, English presentations, category assignments and selection probabilities are adaptations. A citation establishes the component evidence, not that an entire generated name appears in a book.

Examples: **Gorim Durazklinka** combines an attested forename (DPG p.39) with stone + chisel (pp.44,46); the complete name is adapted. **Kolklinka** is a black + chisel symbolic label (p.46). **Karak Kolgrongkhaz** is an adapted hold proposal from the attested Karak place-name pattern and black + anvil + hall roots (pp.45–46). `Karak` literally has the selected sense “enduring”; the interface's “hold of” is an English presentation, not a dictionary quotation. Forenames have no invented English translations.

Source references in runtime output identify the selected evidence. Contextual references and collision reservations are not blanket permission to invent new vocabulary. Prior collision research used a broader corpus only as an exclusion aid; fan material was not a vocabulary authority. Screening is bounded by source coverage and OCR quality and cannot establish that no generated name appears anywhere in print.

No book scans, OCR corpus or long extracts belong in the player package. The game does not contact a lore service. A player does not need to own or install a research database to run the mod.

## Meaning safeguards

A symbolic family label establishes no profession, deed, parentage or clan membership. A location motif establishes no mineral deposit, founder or owner. A proposed geographical name does not create the corresponding feature. Earned epithets and caregiver surnames need facts the generator does not have and are excluded. Item-specific concrete words require observed item evidence; symbolic words do not grant magic.

Warhammer and Khazalid originate in the cited publishers' works. Citation and fan-project status do not settle redistribution rights; see [provenance](PROVENANCE.md).
